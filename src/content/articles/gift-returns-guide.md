Returning a gift is a small social puzzle: you want to swap it without the giver finding out, and you usually don't have the receipt. Stores solve this with gift receipts, which prove the item came from them without showing the price to you.

## What a gift receipt gets you

A gift receipt protects the buyer's payment, so the person receiving the gift is usually offered an **exchange or store credit** rather than a cash refund. Some real examples:

- [Ulta Beauty](/stores/ulta/): exchange or merchandise credit within 60 days with a gift receipt.
- [L.L.Bean](/stores/ll-bean/): merchandise credit within a year with a gift receipt.
- [Primark](/stores/primark/): a gift receipt gets you an exchange.
- [Macy's](/stores/macys/): gift registry items can be returned within 90 days after the event date, with a receipt.

## No gift receipt?

You still have options, but expect credit at a lower price. [Kohl's](/stores/kohls/) gives merchandise credit at the lowest sale price of the last 13 weeks when it can't find the purchase, and [Macy's](/stores/macys/) gives store credit at the item's lowest recent selling price. Ask the person who gave it whether they paid by card or with a loyalty account; many stores can look the sale up. See [returning without a receipt](/guides/return-without-receipt/).

## How to return a gift smoothly

1. **Don't open or wear it** if you think you'll return it; condition rules still apply to gifts.
2. **Find out where it came from.** Marketplace items on Amazon or Walmart may go back to a third-party seller.
3. **Bring the gift receipt** or the order details the giver shares with you.
4. **Choose an exchange** if the store offers credit only: you'll get full value straight away.

## If you're the one giving

Ask for a gift receipt at the till or tick the gift option online. It costs nothing and saves the recipient from asking you for the receipt later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get cash back for a gift?

Usually not. With a gift receipt, stores typically offer an exchange or store credit so the buyer's payment isn't refunded to someone else.

### Will the giver find out I returned it?

Gift receipt returns are designed so the recipient gets credit without the buyer being involved, though store practices vary.

### How long do I have to return a Christmas gift?

It depends on the store: many extended windows end in January 2027. Check the holiday deadlines list or the store's own page.
