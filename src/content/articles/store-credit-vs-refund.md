A refund puts money back on your card. Store credit ties it to one shop, sometimes only online or only in stores, and sometimes with an expiry date. Which one you get usually depends on a single number: how many days have passed since you bought the item.

## The refund cliff: same store, two deadlines

Many stores have a refund window followed by a credit window. Miss the first and your cash turns into credit:

| Store | Refund until | Then |
|---|---|---|
| [Ulta Beauty](/stores/ulta/) | Day 30 | Merchandise credit until day 60 |
| [American Eagle](/stores/american-eagle/) | Day 30 (online +7) | Merchandise credit until day 60 |
| [Urban Outfitters](/stores/urban-outfitters/) | Day 30 after delivery | UO gift card for the price paid |
| [Zappos](/stores/zappos/) | Day 60 | Store credit up to a year |
| [Bath & Body Works](/stores/bath-and-body-works/) | Day 90 | Merchandise credit |
| [Kmart Australia](/stores/kmart-au/) (OnePass) | Day 60 | Exchange voucher until day 365 |
| [Target Australia](/stores/target-au/) (OnePass) | Day 60 | Target Returns Card until day 365 |

The lesson is simple: if you're unsure about something, decide early.

## Late returns and vouchers

Some stores accept late returns but only as a voucher. [Next](/stores/next/) gives an eVoucher valid for six months for returns after its window. That's better than nothing, but check the expiry before you leave the counter.

## Credit by choice: when credit is the better deal

Sometimes credit saves you money. [Patagonia](/stores/patagonia/) waives its $7 return label fee if you choose merchandise credit instead of a refund. If you were going to shop there again anyway, the credit is worth more.

## Credit by policy: exchange-only stores

Some brands don't refund change-of-mind returns at all. In Pakistan, [Outfitters](/stores/outfitters/) and [Limelight](/stores/limelight/) give exchanges, a credit note or a discount code, and [Sapphire](/stores/sapphire/) usually settles returns as store credit. See the full list of [exchange-only stores](/guides/exchange-only-stores/).

## Where store credit can be spent

Credit isn't always usable everywhere in the same brand. [Sephora's](/stores/sephora/) store credit is usable in Sephora stores only, and its online credit only on Sephora.com. Ask before you accept it.

## How to keep your refund

1. Return inside the refund window, not just the credit window.
2. Keep proof of purchase; no-receipt returns almost always become credit.
3. Return in the original condition, with tags.
4. Ask whether a refund to your card is possible before accepting credit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is store credit the same as a refund?

No. A refund goes back to your original payment method; store credit can only be spent at that store, sometimes only in one channel.

### Why did I get store credit instead of my money back?

Usually because the return was after the refund window, there was no proof of purchase, it was a gift receipt return, or the store gives credit by policy.

### Can I turn store credit into cash?

Generally not. Treat credit as money you'll spend at that store.
